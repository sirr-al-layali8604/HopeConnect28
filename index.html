About HopeConnect

An initiative by Bhavna Trust and its students.

Who we are

HopeConnect is an initiative by Bhavna Trust and its students.

Why we exist

Families in Greater Mumbai, Thane and Palghar often wait for help because the people who can give it never hear about the need. HopeConnect puts every request, contribution and status change on one list, so verified NGOs, volunteers and donors act on the same facts.

1

Ask

A seeker describes the need and gets a tracking ID straight away.

2

Review

An NGO admin checks the case, then assigns a volunteer.

3

Follow

Status moves from submitted to completed, and donors see where funds went.

What we promise

  • Every request is reviewed by an NGO admin before it is assigned.
  • Each contribution gets a reference ID in the ledger.
  • Each dashboard opens only for its own role. Headline numbers stay public on the gateway.
  • Contact numbers never appear on public screens.
